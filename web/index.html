Dirt Bike

After Dirt Bike 4.3 by Brad Quick & Michel de Messieres (1998)
–
Throttle is the height of the mouse: top = full throttle, below the dashed line = brake. Up/Down keys also work. In the air, throttle up lifts the nose, throttle down drops it. → / click: shift up (also starts the engine), ←: shift down. R: restart, Esc: menu, P: pause. Blue bikes are recorded high scores from the original track files, green is your best run.

Values are the game's internal units (mm, kg, degrees; power/brake/gears unitless).
